**Implementable access paths**

- **Leaguepedia:** [Cargo API probe](https://lol.fandom.com/api.php?action=cargoquery&format=json&tables=ScoreboardGames&fields=GameId,OverviewPage&limit=1). Query `ScoreboardGames`, `ScoreboardTeams` and `ScoreboardPlayers` separately; join through `GameId`. Resolve tournaments through `OverviewPage`, not the deprecated free-text `Tournament` field. Player `Link` includes disambiguation; `Items` uses semicolons. The API probe was inaccessible here. [Game schema](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Module%3ACargoDeclare/ScoreboardGames), [player schema](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Module%3ACargoDeclare/ScoreboardPlayers), [team/objective schema](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Module%3ACargoDeclare/ScoreboardTeams).
- **Oracle’s Elixir:** start at its [download page](https://oracleselixir.com/tools/downloads). Candidate direct files: [2014 CSV](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=12syQsRH2QnKrQZTQQ6G5zyVeTG2pAYvu), [2025 CSV](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1v6LRphp2kYciU4SXp0PCjEMuev1bDejc). These identifiers came from a [downstream mapping](https://github.com/HerrKurz/Esports_Data_Pipeline/blob/master/config.py), so **verify ownership, filename and contents before ingestion**.
- **GRID:** `GET https://api.grid.gg/file-download/list/{seriesId}`, then download the returned `fullURL` for events or end-state. Requires `x-api-key`; no credentials were accessed. [Provider instructions](https://grid.helpjuice.com/how-do-i-use-the-file-download-api?kb_language=en_US).
- **Riot’s public Match API:** do not assume it can reconstruct historical tournament-realm Worlds. Riot’s published 2019 policy described rolling two-year match and one-year timeline retention; that is historical documentation, not a freshly verified retention guarantee. [Riot notice](https://www.riotgames.com/en/DevRel/match-history-retention-Change).

**Bounded calculation plan**

1. **Create a 2011–2025 coverage manifest first.** Explicitly include play-ins, main event and tiebreakers; exclude qualifiers. Record expected played games, source IDs, stage, series/game number, patch, draft rules, field availability and unresolved gaps. Distinguish played games from awarded wins, remakes and abandoned attempts.
2. **Pilot 2011, 2013 and 2025 before expanding.** Retrieve records in stable ordered pages; retain source URL, retrieval date, schema version and content hash. Validate one winner, two teams and ten participants per ordinary completed game. Reconcile every included game against a second schedule source. Preserve unknown, unavailable-in-this-patch and observed-zero as different states.
3. **Calculate only validated denominators.** Champion pick rate = games containing the champion ÷ games with complete drafts. Champion win rate = wins ÷ appearances with known results. Team/player win rates use their own game appearances. Show counts and excluded records alongside rates.
4. **Handle Fearless separately.** For 2025, retain series order and prior picks; publish ordinary pick rate plus an explicitly defined availability-adjusted rate. Prior-series picks remove champions from later games under Fearless. [Riot’s rule explanation](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fearless-draft-takes-over-2025).
5. **Pilot objective trades on one complete series with timelines.** Define a candidate trade as opposing teams taking specified objectives within 60 seconds of game time; test 30/90-second windows. Record tower lane/tier, dragon subtype, Herald/Baron and other patch-valid objectives. Prevent duplicate pairing and flag ambiguous sequences. These are temporal exchanges, not proof of deliberate strategic trades. **Final objective totals cannot establish them.**
6. **Keep historical findings patch-specific.** Store patch as text, version champion/item identities, and use patch-matched static data. Never price old objectives using current rewards or interpret final inventory as purchase order. Riot provides [versioned Data Dragon resources](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol). Display historical rates as historical evidence; require separate current-patch evidence for coaching recommendations.

The immediate deliverable should be the **coverage manifest and validated pilot**, with objective-trade analysis disabled wherever timelines are missing. Exact missing games and field-complete years remain unresolved until those downloads succeed.
