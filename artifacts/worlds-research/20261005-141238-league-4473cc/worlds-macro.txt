The following are **proposed testable rules**, not validated detectors. Each call is short enough for approximately ten seconds of speech.

| Pattern | Observable trigger and cancellation condition | Voice call | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wave crash → roam** | Wave actually reaches enemy tower; teammate can support the destination. Cancel if the crash fails or the route becomes unsafe. | “Crash mid, then move with jungle. Leave the play when their mid can follow.” | Did movement follow a real crash? Record pressure gained and the return-wave cost. |
| **Side-lane pressure** | Side wave threatens a structure; teammates can avoid engagement. Cancel when defenders disappear toward the split-pusher. | “Keep pushing bot. Four of us hold safely; back off when their cover disappears.” | Defender diverted, structure damage gained, deaths conceded. |
| **Mid priority** | Allied mid can leave first after clearing, with sufficient health and mana. Cancel if adjacent enemies can arrive sooner. | “Clear mid together, then enter their jungle. Stop if their side lane moves first.” | Compare actual arrival order; count unsupported entries. |
| **Cross-map tower trade** | Enemy commitment is visible elsewhere; allied wave reaches a vulnerable tower; base remains defensible. | “They’re committed top. Take bot tower, then reset before they rotate.” | Tower secured before collapse, against structures and lives lost elsewhere. |
| **Jungle tempo** | Enemy jungler is observed spending time elsewhere; reachable camps and lane support are known. Cancel speculative invasions into unseen camps. | “Their jungler showed top. Take the safe bottom camps, then leave together.” | Resources gained minus travel time, lost farm and deaths. |
| **Vision trap** | Swept approach, nearby follow-up, catch tools available, safe exit. Cancel if spotted or backup disappears. | “Sweep this entrance and wait together. Catch the first face-check; don’t chase beyond vision.” | Whether the trap stayed concealed, produced a catch, or wasted time. |

Evidence is strongest for **jungle tempo, side pressure and vision relocation**. Crash-to-roam has a detailed but amateur recent source. A strictly verified **tower-for-tower trade remains a gap**. The recent sample ends in 2023; it makes no coverage claim about 2024–2025.
