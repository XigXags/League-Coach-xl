# Worlds research for League Coach

System Administrator ran a read-only, three-part research batch on October 5, 2026. Its reports are in [artifacts/worlds-research/20261005-141238-league-4473cc](artifacts/worlds-research/20261005-141238-league-4473cc). The workers audited data sources, read a small set of match analyses, and checked current rules. They did **not** watch every Worlds game or compute historical win rates. No VOD frames were personally inspected, so this is a source audit and play-design starting point rather than a trained model.

## What the sample supports

Professional teams repeatedly create a lane or information advantage, use it elsewhere, and leave before the response arrives. The reviewed examples include Samsung White versus Star Horn Royal Club in 2014 for jungle tempo and vision relocation; G2 versus SKT in 2019 for side pressure and an opposite-side structure trade; and T1 matches in 2023 for mid-priority into jungle entry and protection against the reply. These conclusions come from named match analyses and metadata, with their individual limits recorded in [the macro report](artifacts/worlds-research/20261005-141238-league-4473cc/worlds-macro.txt). They are design leads, not frequency estimates.

For a general “what now?” question, the coach now offers distinct lane and structure routes: mid shove into one river entrance, side tower pressure with four holding mid, and a Baron setup only after its spawn window. It no longer inserts Grubs and Dragon as the default pair. Jev ranks these routes; a diversity check prevents both spoken choices from being near-identical mid-river plans. Explicit objective comparisons still compare the objectives the team asked about.

## Data path for a full historical study

[Leaguepedia](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Season_1_World_Championship/Match_History) has historical game records; [Oracle's Elixir downloads](https://oracleselixir.com/tools/downloads) are a candidate for bulk match statistics; [Games of Legends](https://gol.gg/tournament/tournament-matchlist/Worlds%202025%20Main%20Event/) provides match tables. Coverage and field completeness across every year have **not** been verified. The System Administrator [data report](artifacts/worlds-research/20261005-141238-league-4473cc/worlds-data.txt) proposes first building a 2011–2025 game manifest, validating records from early, middle and recent years, and calculating pick or win rates only over complete, patch-labeled denominators. Final match stats cannot reconstruct a wave crash, jungle path or timed objective trade without event or video evidence.

Historical Worlds play spans major patch and draft-rule changes. Use it to discover and test play patterns; do not treat an old item win rate or objective value as current guidance. Current timing and data-field checks are in [the rules report](artifacts/worlds-research/20261005-141238-league-4473cc/current-rules.txt), with primary [Riot patch notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) and [Riot's local API documentation](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol). The current bot has no reliable live wave coordinates, enemy last-seen locations or ordinary jungle-camp events, so it does not claim a lane crash, invade opportunity or safe fog route as observed fact.
