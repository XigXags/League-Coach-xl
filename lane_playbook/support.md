# Support

## Summary

The support decides what the team can see and whether a move is safe. Their job is vision, peel, and the engage that starts a fight the team can win. A support who places wards early, sweeps before objectives, and stays with the marksman protects the lane and the map. Support is also the lane's second body: trades with the bot lane depend on their level and spells. Check the enemy support's spells, whether the lane is pushed toward you, and whether your bot can afford to leave the wave.

## Job

- Give the team vision: river, lane bushes, and the path to the next objective.
- Protect the bot laner in lane and in fights; peel or engage when the team can follow.
- Set up objectives with the jungler and bot, not alone.
- Keep the marksman's wave safe and trade when the enemy support is caught out.

## Mechanics

- Vision: wards take time and are lost quickly, so placement matters. A ward placed where the enemy walks is more valuable than a ward placed where it is easy to see.
- Sweeping: clearing enemy wards before an objective is the difference between a safe setup and a trap. Sweep before the spawn, not during the fight.
- Peel and engage: peel protects the marksman by stopping enemy access to them. Engage starts a fight by stunning or displacing the enemy. Choose the one the team needs.
- Level spikes: support champions depend on a power spike at a level breakpoint. A trade that uses the spike wins the lane.
- Lane positioning: a support who walks too far from the wave gives the enemy a free trade.

## Early (lane phase)

- Ward the river and the lane bush that the enemy jungler uses to arrive.
- Stay with the bot laner. A support who leaves the wave gives the enemy support or jungler a free kill.
- Trade the enemy support's key spell. If they have it up and you do not, hold near the wave.
- Level 2 all-in only when both key spells are ready and the enemy support is caught out of position.

## Mid game

- Sweep and ward before the next objective. Place vision on the entrance to the pit, not inside it.
- Rotate with the jungler and mid to set up dragon or Herald. A support who is alone at the river is a target.
- Use the wave and the vision together: a bot who has the wave and a support who has vision can take a tower safely.

## Late game

- Vision for Baron or Elder is the team's first priority. Place wards on the approach and sweep before the fight.
- Engage only with the team behind you. An engage without a follow-up is a free kill for the enemy.
- Peel the carry: stay between the marksman and the enemy's dive.

## Matchups

- Against an engage support: keep your bot lane positioned behind you, and do not walk into their combo alone.
- Against a poke support: keep the wave close to your tower and trade only when their spell is down.
- Against a lane bully support: keep your bot laner's health high and use vision on the river to see the jungler coming.

## Reading the feed

- Deaths and respawn timers show who is available to engage or peel.
- The feed does not show ward coverage. A support's vision placement must be stated as a plan, not as a known fact.
- Objective events show the buff state. They do not show where enemies are standing.

## Common mistakes

- Warding too far from the path the enemy will walk.
- Leaving the bot laner alone in lane.
- Starting an engage without a follow-up.
- Skipping the sweep before an objective spawn.
- Roaming away from the marksman when the wave needs protection.

## What a useful call names

- The vision target: which river, bush, or pit, and when it is needed.
- The peel or engage target and the marksman it protects.
- The lane or objective the support is setting up for.
- The exit: when to leave the area or stop warding.
- The fallback if the enemy jungler or support arrives first.
