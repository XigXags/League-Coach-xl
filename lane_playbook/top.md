# Top lane

## Summary

Top is a single-lane role that often plays alone on a side. The top laner's job is to hold a wave that gives them space, decide whether to fight, trade, or split, and avoid losing tempo to a lone ganker. Top decides side-lane pressure: a top laner who can keep a wave near their tower can force the enemy to respond. Check the enemy top's presence, teleport use, and whether the jungler can reach top before any push. Never commit to a split when the enemy has more numbers on the side.

## Job

- Keep the top wave in a shape you can use: near your tower for safety, or pushed for a recall, roam, or tower hit.
- Choose the lane's tempo: when to trade, when to hold, when to step back.
- Provide side pressure when mid and bot can hold the centre.
- Use teleport or the jungle to join a fight or an objective when the top wave allows.

## Mechanics

- Melee versus ranged: a ranged top laner who pokes a melee one can keep the melee from farming, but the melee keeps the wave in range if it is patient. Count the enemy's reach before trading.
- Sustain versus burst: a sustained champion wins long trades; a burst champion wins short ones. Pick trades that match your champion.
- Wave control: a freeze near your tower protects your CS and forces the enemy to walk in. A push forward is a commitment to the next minute.
- Level spikes: most top champions get a key power at a level breakpoint. Trades near that breakpoint decide the lane.

## Early (lane phase)

- Hold the first wave until the jungler's first clear or the first safe approach is known.
- If the enemy top has their key spell down, trade. If they have it up and you do not, hold the wave near your tower.
- Teleport is a resource. Do not spend it on a wave you cannot convert.
- Side wave: if the enemy top is far forward, the side wave can be pushed into their tower for a free tower hit when the enemy has no response.

## Mid game

- After the lane, top can split when the enemy has no one close enough to respond. A split is only worth it if mid and bot hold the centre and the jungler can reach the top side.
- Tower trades: a wave cleared under the enemy tower gives a free hit. Leave before the enemy responds.
- Keep teleport for objective setup or a fight that matters, not for a quick farm trip.

## Late game

- Top is often isolated in fights. Stand where your team can join you, not where you can be picked off alone.
- Teleport in after the fight starts, not before. Arriving late from the side is a common way to lose a fight that was already decided.
- If the enemy top is missing, the side is open for a moment. Use the time for a tower or to group with the team, not for a solo chase.

## Matchups

- Against an engage top: keep the wave near your tower and preserve your key cooldowns. Trade after they commit.
- Against a split pusher: keep your jungler informed and hold the top river. A split pusher on a side with no vision is a target.
- Against a ranged top laner in a long lane: step out of range and hold the wave where your teammates can see it.

## Reading the feed

- Top's level and CS lead against the enemy top are clues, not wave positions.
- A top laner's death and the respawn timer change the side's numbers immediately. Use them to decide whether a split is safe.
- A missing enemy top laner usually means a roam, a recall, or a teleport. Treat that as likely, not confirmed.

## Common mistakes

- Walking under an enemy tower without the wave.
- Splitting into four or five players.
- Trading when the enemy's key spell is ready and yours is not.
- Teleporting into a fight that is already lost.
- Letting the wave push into an enemy tower and leaving it there.

## What a useful call names

- The top wave's state and where it sits.
- Whether the jungler can reach top, and the path.
- Whether to trade, hold, or split, with the spell or teleport state the team can see.
- The exit deadline and the fallback if the enemy top does not appear.
