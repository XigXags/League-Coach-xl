# Bot lane (marksman)

## Summary

Bot is the scaling role. The marksman's job is to farm safely, hit damage in the right moment, and stay alive into the fight. They win through positioning: standing behind the frontline, choosing targets the enemy cannot easily protect, and leaving a fight before the enemy resets. Bot lane is a two-person lane with the support, so wave, trade, and spell timing are shared. Check the enemy support's spells and the enemy's lane position before trading. Never fight when the enemy's lock-down or teleport can catch you out of position.

## Job

- Farm safely: keep the wave in a place where you can last-hit without taking extra damage.
- Trade with the support when the enemy's support is not ready to punish.
- Protect the damage you need later: a marksman who dies early loses the late game.
- Fight from the back line in team fights and leave before the enemy resets.

## Mechanics

- Wave control with the support: the support's level and the lane's position decide when you can trade. A wave pushed toward you is a safer farm; a wave pushed toward the enemy is a trap.
- Range: marksmen have long range and fragile bodies. Stay within range of the wave and out of range of the enemy engage.
- Positioning: the best position is behind the frontline and within reach of your targets. Stepping forward for a few last hits costs more than it gives in a fight.
- Trades: trade when the enemy support's key spell is down, or when they are caught out of position. An all-in with the support needs a clear kill window.
- Scaling: a marksman's damage grows with items. Early death is the largest loss.

## Early (lane phase)

- Keep the wave pushed toward your side when the enemy support can engage on you. Freeze near your tower when your support is not ready to protect you.
- Trade the enemy support's first key spell. If they have it up, step back and farm.
- Recall or back when the wave is pushed and the enemy has no response. Do not back in a wave that is about to be trapped.
- Do not walk far from the wave for a fight that your team cannot back up.

## Mid game

- Rotate with the support to objectives when the wave is pushed. Leave the lane with a clear wave, not a clear wave under the enemy tower.
- Take tower plates when the lane is safe. Plates are a source of gold and a sign that the lane is yours.
- Group for dragon or Herald with the team. A marksman alone at the river is a target.

## Late game

- Your job is damage. Stand behind the frontline and attack the targets the enemy cannot protect.
- Track the enemy's key lock-downs and teleports. Do not stand where a single spell ends your fight.
- Leave fights before the enemy resets. A marksman who stays alone in a fight that is already lost loses the team a respawn timer's worth of pressure.

## Matchups

- Against an engage support: keep your health high and avoid trades that the support can punish with a combo. Let your jungler or mid help before trading.
- Against a poke support: trade when they have spent their key spell, and keep the wave away from their poke range.
- Against a melee bot: use your range and the wave. Melee bots need to walk into you.

## Reading the feed

- Deaths and respawn timers show the lane's numbers. A dead enemy bot is an opening for a push.
- Items show your progress. They do not show the enemy's spell readiness.
- CS and gold leads are clues. They do not prove that the wave is safe or that a fight will go your way.

## Common mistakes

- Standing in front of the frontline in a team fight.
- Greedy CS while the team is fighting in the river.
- Trading when the support's engage is up and yours is down.
- Backing into an enemy tower's range with a pushed wave.
- Staying in a fight after the enemy has reset.

## What a useful call names

- The lane's wave state and whether the support is in position.
- The trade or fight, and the enemy support's spell state the team can see.
- The positioning: where to stand behind the frontline and what target to focus.
- The exit: when to leave, and where to reset or back.
- The fallback if the enemy support or teammate does not commit.
