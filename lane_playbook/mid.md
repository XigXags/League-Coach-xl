# Mid lane

## Summary

Mid is the lane that connects the map. A mid laner who controls the wave can move to the side, join a fight, or set up an objective, and the enemy must respond to where they are. Mid decides roam windows: a mid wave pushed to tower gives a short window to move, and the window closes when the wave is cleared or the enemy responds. The mid laner must return before the wave resets. Check the enemy mid's position, the jungler's path, and whether a roam is backed by a plan.

## Job

- Control the mid wave so it creates a usable window: a freeze for safety, a shove for a roam, or a crash for a recall.
- Trade in mid when the trade is favourable. Do not trade just to trade.
- Set up roams and objectives with the jungler and support.
- Stay alive and in position so the team can use mid's roam or its vision.

## Mechanics

- Wave control: a wave that reaches the enemy tower and is cleared gives a short window. A wave that is only half pushed gives the enemy a free freeze.
- Shove and move: shove the wave, then move to the side with the wave still near tower so the enemy cannot clear it. The window is only as long as the wave survives.
- Range and burst: mid champions differ in reach and burst. A ranged mid with a long poke wins trades at range; a burst mid wins by picking the moment.
- Level spikes: mid champions usually depend on a power spike at a level breakpoint and on their key item. Trades before the spike favour the player who has it first.
- Mid priority: winning the mid lane lets you move first. The lane must be actually won, not just the CS.

## Early (lane phase)

- Trade when the enemy mid's key spell is down. Hold the wave when the enemy jungler is near and the wave can be pushed into them.
- Ward the mid river and the nearest brush so you see the jungler's approach. Vision protects the wave.
- A roam before level 3 or before the first back is usually early. Roam only with a wave that is safe and a jungler who is coming.
- Give up a CS to keep health. A dead mid laner loses the next wave and the roam.

## Mid game

- Roam to a side when the wave is at tower and the enemy side laner is not ready. Return before the enemy responds.
- Rotate to the objective with the jungler and support. The mid laner's presence can make the entrance safe.
- Use the tower plates and the mid wave to create vision on the approach to the next objective.
- If the enemy mid is missing, the centre is open. Shove and push a tower instead of hunting.

## Late game

- Mid is the protector of the carries in a fight. Stand where you can join the fight without being the first target.
- Keep the mid wave near your tower late in the game when the enemy has more numbers. A wave that is pushed into a trap costs more than it gives.
- Use teleport or flash to join the fight after the first engage, not before.

## Matchups

- Against a poke mid: keep the wave near your tower and avoid long trades that favour their range.
- Against an assassin mid: keep your key spell for the moment they commit, and watch the jungler's approach.
- Against a roamer: use the wave to pressure their side and punish their absence.

## Reading the feed

- A mid laner's death changes the wave and the roam window. Count the team's living players before choosing.
- The enemy mid's missing presence means a roam or recall is likely, but the feed does not show where they are.
- CS and level leads are clues to strength, not to the wave's position.

## Common mistakes

- Roaming with the wave not at tower.
- Crashing the wave with no return plan.
- Walking into the river alone when the jungler is missing.
- Trading when the enemy has their spell up and yours is down.
- Staying in lane when the team needs a roam.

## What a useful call names

- The wave state and the point where it is pushed.
- The roam target and the path, with the jungler or support who will join.
- The exit deadline: when the wave resets or the enemy responds.
- The fallback if the expected roam is not safe.
